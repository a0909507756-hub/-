### 1. 【The LEGO Assembly Line】
- **Questions:** 
  * What type of bond links amino acids together to form a protein? 
  * Which amino acids form hydrogen bonds with each other?
- **Answer:**
  * **Bond linking amino acids:** **Peptide bond** (a covalent amide bond formed via dehydration synthesis/condensation between the carboxyl group $\text{-COOH}$ of one amino acid and the amino group $\text{-NH}_2$ of another).  
    *（中文：胜肽鍵／肽鍵。由一個胺基酸的羧基與另一個胺基酸的胺基透過脫水縮合反應形成的共價鍵。）*
  * **Amino acids that form hydrogen bonds:** 
    1. **Backbone interactions:** **All amino acids** can form hydrogen bonds with each other through their backbone carbonyl oxygen ($\text{-C=O}$) and amide hydrogen ($\text{-N-H}$) groups, which stabilize secondary structures ($\alpha$-helices and $\beta$-sheets).
    2. **Side-chain (R-group) interactions:** Amino acids with **polar uncharged side chains** (**Ser, Thr, Tyr, Asn, Gln, Cys**) and **charged/ionizable side chains** (**Asp, Glu, Lys, Arg, His**, as well as **Trp**) can form hydrogen bonds via the oxygen (O), nitrogen (N), or sulfur (S) atoms in their side chains.  
    *（中文：1. 所有胺基酸的主鏈之間皆可透過羰基氧與亞胺基氫形成氫鍵；2. 具極性側鏈的胺基酸，包含極性不帶電的 Ser、Thr、Tyr、Asn、Gln、Cys，以及帶電荷的 Asp、Glu、Lys、Arg、His 與 Trp，其側鏈之間皆可形成氫鍵。）*

### 2. 【The Double-Track Highway of Life’s Blueprint】
- **Questions:** 
  * How do nucleotides assemble to form double-stranded DNA? 
  * What are the basic structural components of DNA? 
  * Is DNA right-handed or left-handed? 
  * List the names of the four DNA bases. 
  * Which bases form hydrogen bonds with each other?
- **Answer:**
  * **Assembly into double-stranded DNA:** Adjacent nucleotides on a single strand are linked by covalent **phosphodiester bonds** (between the 3'-OH group of one deoxyribose sugar and the 5'-phosphate group of the next) to form a sugar-phosphate backbone. Two antiparallel strands (one 5'→3' and the other 3'→5') then twist around each other into a double helix held together by **hydrogen bonds** between complementary inward-facing bases and stabilized by base-stacking interactions.  
    *（中文：單股內相鄰核苷酸透過 3' 碳羥基與 5' 碳磷酸基形成共價「磷酸二酯鍵」構成五碳醣-磷酸骨架；兩股反向平行的單股再透過內側互補鹼基之間的「氫鍵」配對與鹼基堆疊力，組裝成雙股螺旋。）*
  * **Basic structural components of DNA:** The basic unit is a **deoxyribonucleotide**, which consists of three components: a **5-carbon deoxyribose sugar**, a **phosphate group**, and a **nitrogenous base**.  
    *（中文：基本單位為去氧核糖核苷酸，由五碳醣［去氧核糖］、磷酸基團與含氮鹼基三部分組成。）*
  * **Handedness of DNA:** Standard physiological DNA (B-DNA) is **right-handed**.  
    *（中文：一般生理狀態下的標準 DNA［B-DNA］為右旋。）*
  * **Four DNA bases:** **Adenine (A)**, **Thymine (T)**, **Guanine (G)**, and **Cytosine (C)**.  
    *（中文：腺嘌呤 A、胸腺嘧啶 T、鳥糞嘌呤 G、胞嘧啶 C。）*
  * **Base hydrogen bonding (Complementary base pairing):** **Adenine (A) pairs with Thymine (T)** via **2 hydrogen bonds** ($\text{A=T}$), and **Guanine (G) pairs with Cytosine (C)** via **3 hydrogen bonds** ($\text{G}\equiv\text{C}$).  
    *（中文：A 與 T 配對形成 2 個氫鍵；G 與 C 配對形成 3 個氫鍵。）*

### 4. 【Return to the Crime Scene】
- **Question:** Why can a mutation (change) in just one amino acid of a protein sometimes lead to a serious human disease? Give one specific example of such a disease.
- **Answer:**
  * **Explanation:** A protein's biological function depends strictly on its precise 3D structure and the chemical properties (charge, polarity, size, and hydrophobicity) of its amino acid residues. A single amino acid substitution (missense mutation) can disrupt critical hydrogen bonds, ionic interactions, or hydrophobic packing. This can either **directly destroy an active/DNA-binding site** or **cause the entire protein domain to misfold and lose stability**, abolishing its normal physiological function.  
    *（中文：蛋白質的功能高度依賴其精確的三維結構與胺基酸側鏈的化學性質［電荷、極性、大小、疏水性］。單一胺基酸突變可能破壞關鍵的氫鍵或離子鍵，進而直接摧毀結合活性位點，或導致蛋白質錯誤摺疊、失去結構穩定性而喪失功能。）*
  * **Specific Examples:**
    1. **Li-Fraumeni Syndrome / Human Cancers (TP53 mutation):** A single missense mutation in TP53 (such as **R248Q**, **R273H**, **R280K**, or **R175H**) prevents TP53 from binding to target DNA to arrest the cell cycle or trigger apoptosis, leading to uncontrolled cell division and cancer.
    2. **Sickle Cell Anemia (鐮刀型紅血球貧血症):** A single point mutation in hemoglobin $\beta$-globin (**Glu6Val / E6V**) replaces a hydrophilic glutamic acid with a hydrophobic valine, causing hemoglobin molecules to stick together into rigid fibers that deform red blood cells into a sickle shape.

- **Answer:**
  * **Identified TP53 Cancer Mutation & 3D Position Analysis (Choose the one matching your ChimeraX screenshot):**

Mutating these residues directly abolishes TP53–DNA binding.  
      *（中文選擇一［接觸型突變］：**Arg280、Arg248 或 Arg273**。從 3KMD 的 3D 結構來看，該胺基酸**直接位於蛋白質–DNA 結合介面［protein–DNA interface］**上，直接參與和 DNA 鹼基或磷酸骨架的氫鍵／靜電作用，突變後會導致 TP53 無法抓緊 DNA。）*

Based on its 3D position in `3KMD`, this residue is located **somewhere else in the protein (within the internal structural core / $\beta$-sandwich / zinc-binding region, away from the direct DNA-contact surface)**. Mutating it destabilizes and unfolds the 3D structure of the TP53 DNA-binding domain, indirectly preventing DNA binding.  
      *（中文選擇二［結構型突變］：**Arg175、Tyr220 或 Gly245**。從 3KMD 的 3D 結構來看，該胺基酸**位於蛋白質內部結構區［而非直接接觸 DNA 的介面］**，突變後會破壞 TP53 結構域的穩定性並導致蛋白質構型瓦解，間接使其喪失結合 DNA 的能力。）*
